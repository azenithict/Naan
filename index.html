What is the difference between the two breads?

Our white homestyle bread is made without added bran and costs $4.25 CAD. Our brown homestyle bread includes bran and costs $4.50 CAD. Both have the familiar flat, oval shape and toasted surface of traditional local-style bread.

Can I choose pickup or delivery?

Yes. Choose pickup or request delivery when you send your order request. Pickup is at 220 6th St, New Westminster, BC V3L 3A2. Baanou will confirm timing, delivery availability and any delivery fee directly with you.

How do I know my order is confirmed?

After you submit, your request is emailed to Baanou and you receive a reference number on screen. It becomes a confirmed order only after the store contacts you to confirm availability, timing and any applicable extra charges.

Do I need to pay online?

No online payment is taken on this site. Baanou will confirm payment arrangements directly with you.

What about allergens?

These breads contain wheat. Please contact Baanou about other ingredients and cross-contact before ordering if you have an allergy or dietary requirement.
